Privacy policy

Effective: October 19, 2026.

1. What data

Analytics tools — Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC, Russia) and Google Analytics (Google) — use cookies to record the IP address, browser and device details, where the visit came from, clicks and scrolling. Both are loaded through Google Tag Manager, which itself sets no cookies. If you choose to leave your email address on the payment page, it is stored in Yandex Forms (Yandex LLC, Russia). The site collects no other data. The site host (GitHub Pages, GitHub, Inc., USA) technically processes the IP address.

2. Purpose

To count which features and plans visitors are interested in before the service is built; the email address — to send one message when the service starts, with your consent given by submitting the field.

3. Where data goes

Data may be transferred to Russia (Yandex) and to the USA (Google, GitHub).

4. How long

Analytics data — according to the tools' settings, at most 14 months; the email address — until the launch message is sent or until the decision not to launch the service.

5. Your choices

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data and withdraw your consent; you can block cookies in your browser — the site works without them. We do not sell personal data.
